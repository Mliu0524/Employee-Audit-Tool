Employee Audit Tool

Business Problem:
The Internal Quality Assurance (QA) team previously stored audit results in Excel spreadsheets. As the files continued to grow, they became slow to open and difficult to maintain. Although workbook tracing was enabled, audit scores could still be modified, creating concerns around data integrity, audit accuracy, and compliance reporting.

Tools Used:
Microsoft SQL Server Management Studio (SSMS)
Tableau
Microsoft Excel

Key Results & Insights:
Following the implementation of the Employee Audit Tool, audit data was centralized within SQL Server. Historical audit questions and scores were stored alongside each employee's records in a dedicated history table, creating a permanent audit trail and preventing unauthorized score changes outside of the formal dispute process.
Because audits are performed weekly, the Internal QA team can identify issues more quickly, provide timely feedback to employees, and deliver accurate scores that directly impact employee incentive and bonus programs.
Within three months of implementation, the compliance rate increased by 40%. The tool also helped identify process gaps, enabling corrective actions and operational improvements. Additionally, compliance issues, such as loans that had not been properly worked or followed up on, could be quickly surfaced, monitored, and resolved before becoming larger operational risks.
